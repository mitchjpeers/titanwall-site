Building Features & Benefits

Maximize your space — mezzanines with Titanwall MGO SIP panels.

2-ply structural lumber at 4-foot centres provides more attachment opportunities.

Mezzanine installation — more attachment points with Titanwall™

Unlike traditional pole buildings, where structural posts are commonly spaced 6–12 feet apart, Titanwall MGO SIP panels incorporate 2-ply structural lumber at 4-foot centres.

This provides more frequent locations for securely bolting a mezzanine ledger to the wall, offering greater flexibility for mezzanines, storage and shelving.

With properly engineered connections, a mezzanine can be supported without automatically requiring large, deep LVL beams along the wall.

More attachment points. Greater flexibility. Better use of your space.

Mezzanine connections and load requirements are subject to structural engineering approval.
