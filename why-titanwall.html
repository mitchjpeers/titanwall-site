Foundations

Frost wall vs poured concrete.

The same panel system works below grade. Compared with a poured concrete foundation, the MGO SIP frost wall removes most of the wet trades from the critical path.

Titanwall MGO SIP frost wall
  • ✓No forming
  • ✓No concrete pumping
  • ✓No cure time
  • ✓Installed in 1 – 2 days
  • ✓Fully insulated system
  • ✓Cleaner job site
  • ✓Reduced weather delays

Case Study

Climate-resilient housing.

Titanwall applied MGO SIP envelopes across the Caribou Homes development, designed for extreme northern climates — homes that withstand heavy snow loads, high winds and extreme cold while delivering energy efficiency and resilience.

The Paskwa wildfire

Approximately 300 structures were lost in the surrounding region. Homes built with the Titanwall MGO SIP envelope system remained standing and undamaged — demonstrating the fire-resilient performance of this building method, and its importance in protecting communities.
