Privacy

Privacy.

What we collect through this website, why, and what we do with it.

Last updated September 30, 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Titanwall MGO, a manufacturer of MGO structural insulated panels based in High River, Alberta. Questions about this policy or your information go to marc@titanwall.com or 403 606-0855.

What we collect when you contact us

When you send an inquiry through the quote form, we receive the details you enter: your name and email address, and, if you choose to give them, your phone number, project type, build location and project details.

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote and follow up on your project. We don’t sell it, rent it, or add you to a mailing list.

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service. We keep inquiries in our business email for as long as we need them to work with you and to keep reasonable business records.

Visitor statistics

We use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count page visits and a few anonymous actions such as quote-button clicks. It sets no cookies, doesn’t identify you, and doesn’t track you across other websites. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, no statistics are collected for your visit.

Content from other services

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google’s servers.
  • Instagram reels load from Instagram only when you choose to play one. Instagram’s own privacy policy applies to that content.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing marc@titanwall.com. We’ll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
