Titan Panel System

One PanelBuilt from Inside Out

Titanwall combines durable MGO sheathing, continuous rigid EPS insulation, and precision lumber connections into one made-to-order panel system designed for efficient site assembly.

  • MGO sheathing

    Inert, virtually VOC-free, and resistant to mould, rot, and pests for a durable exterior surface.

  • Rigid EPS core

    Continuous insulation reduces thermal bridging and supports a tighter, more energy-efficient building envelope.

  • Lumber connections

    Structural splines and plates create strong, precise joining points for accurate panel alignment and assembly.

Base & top plates
Foam is recessed so plates seat flush, with holes pre-drilled to line up the chases.
Electrical chases
Routed through the core at 16″ on centre, so wiring runs without cutting the panel on site.
CNC machined
Openings, chases, splines, and connections are cut to the shop drawings before delivery.
Bevelled edges
Panel joints are bevelled to form a clean caulking groove, making the seal easier to run and inspect.

On Site
